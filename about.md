## About Me

[Find a full version of my CV here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Rvv-GBjlWQjkejzVAFrJ5nDfn0glokuR/view?usp=sharing)

I joined the Department of Biostatistics of the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health as a Research Associate in March 2025. Since then, I'm part of the [NSAPH](https://hsph.harvard.edu/research/air-pollution-health/) group, under the mentorship of [Prof. Francesca Dominici](https://hsph.harvard.edu/profile/francesca-dominici/).

Before moving to the U.S., I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Department of Statistics, Computer Science, Applications "G. Parenti" of the University of Florence. I researched in Florence for 4 years. For the first two years, I was mentored by [Prof. Fabrizia Mealli](https://www.eui.eu/people?id=fabrizia-mealli) (now at the European University Institute), who taught me everything about causal inference. For the second two years, I was an "independent postdoc," PI of the [BayesMeCOS](/bayesmecos/) project funded by NextGenerationEU. During those years, I was also elected as part of the board and then national coordinator of the young section of the Italian Statistical Society ([y-SIS](https://youngsis.github.io/)).

I obtained my PhD in Rome, at Sapienza University, under the supervision of [Prof. Brunero Liseo](https://bruneroliseo.site.uniroma1.it/), who, instead, taught me about Bayesian inference. My PhD thesis is about Bayesian inference for Fisher's noncentral hypergeometric distribution and its applications in population size estimation problems.
